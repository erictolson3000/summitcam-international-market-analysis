# SummitCam International Market Analysis

📄 **Project Report:** [SummitCam_International_Market_Analysis.pdf](SummitCam_International_Market_Analysis.pdf)

---

## Overview

This project evaluates international expansion opportunities for SummitCam, a fictional action camera company. The analysis applies market research, competitive analysis, segmentation, and strategic planning to identify an attractive foreign market and recommend an entry strategy.

---

## Project Objective

Determine the most attractive international market for expansion by evaluating economic conditions, consumer demand, competition, cultural considerations, and market risks.

---

## Methods

- Market Research
- SWOT Analysis
- Competitive Analysis
- Market Segmentation
- Country Evaluation
- International Marketing Strategy

---

## Skills Demonstrated

- Business Analysis
- Market Research
- Competitive Intelligence
- Strategic Planning
- Data-Driven Decision Making
- International Marketing

---

## Recommendation

Based on the analysis, SummitCam should pursue expansion into the selected target market through a localized marketing strategy supported by digital advertising, strategic partnerships, and product positioning tailored to consumer preferences.
